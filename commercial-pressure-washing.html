Commercial Pressure Washing in Destin, FL

Insured commercial exterior cleaning scheduled around your hours, not ours, with recurring plans for properties that need it.

Commercial Exterior Cleaning on Your Schedule

On the Emerald Coast, a customer's first impression is formed in the parking lot. Gum on the sidewalk, a grease trail behind the restaurant, black streaks down a condo building, or a dingy entryway all read as neglect before anyone reaches the door. Powerhouse handles commercial exterior cleaning for retail storefronts, restaurants and their dumpster pads, condo and multi-family buildings, HOA common areas, office parks, and vacation rental portfolios throughout Destin, Miramar Beach and Panama City Beach. We carry full general liability coverage and can provide a certificate of insurance to your property manager before the first visit.

Commercial work runs on your clock. We schedule restaurants and retail before opening or after close, coordinate with property managers on condo buildings so residents get notice, and stage equipment to keep walkways and entrances open while we work. Most commercial clients move to a recurring plan, monthly, quarterly, or twice a year depending on foot traffic and exposure, which keeps the property consistently presentable and costs less per visit than one-off calls. We invoice on net terms and can bill by property for multi-site accounts.
