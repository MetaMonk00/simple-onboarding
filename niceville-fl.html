Pressure Washing in Niceville, FL

Niceville's tree cover is beautiful and it is also why roofs here streak faster than almost anywhere in Okaloosa County.

Your Local pressure washing Team in Niceville

Niceville sits inland on Boggy Bayou, and the tradeoff for being off the open Gulf is shade. Live oaks, water oaks and longleaf pine cover much of Bluewater Bay, Rocky Bayou and the older neighborhoods near the bayou, and that canopy keeps roofs and north walls damp for hours after sunrise. It is the single biggest driver of exterior growth in this area. Roofs under mature canopy in Niceville commonly streak within three years of a new install, and the growth is heavier and more established than what we see on sun-exposed coastal roofs.

The canopy brings a second problem: organic debris. Oak tannin runs off the roof and stains gutter faces, fascia and the concrete below downspouts a rust brown that people often mistake for actual rust. Pine needles and pollen build up in valleys and on flat roof sections, hold water, and accelerate the algae underneath. When we clean a Niceville roof we clear the debris first, then treat, because leaving a valley packed with needles means the treated roof is already re-seeding itself. Newer construction in Deer Moss Creek and Swift Creek has less canopy and correspondingly less growth, but the tannin staining on concrete is still common wherever there are established trees.

Bluewater Bay in particular has homes on golf frontage and heavy irrigation, which brings iron staining onto driveways, walls and the lower courses of siding. That orange staining does not come off with pressure, no matter how much of it you use. It needs a dedicated rust remover applied and neutralized properly, and it is worth pricing separately rather than assuming a standard house wash will handle it.
