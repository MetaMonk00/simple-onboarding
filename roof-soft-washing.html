Roof Soft Washing in Destin, FL

Those black streaks are a living organism eating the limestone in your shingles. We kill it at the root instead of blasting it off.

Why Destin Roofs Streak, and What Actually Fixes It

The dark streaks running down the north and shaded sides of Emerald Coast roofs are almost never dirt. They are Gloeocapsa magma, a hardy blue-green algae that feeds on the limestone filler manufacturers add to asphalt shingles. Our combination of humidity, salt air and long warm seasons makes Destin close to ideal for it. Once it takes hold it spreads across the roof plane, holds moisture against the shingle, and slowly degrades the granules that protect the asphalt underneath. Left alone it shortens roof life and can void some manufacturer warranties.

Pressure washing a shingle roof is the wrong answer. High pressure strips granules, lifts shingle edges, and drives water under the courses and into the decking. Soft washing is what roofing manufacturers and the ARMA actually recommend. We apply a professional cleaning solution at garden-hose pressure, let it dwell long enough to kill the organism through its cell wall, and rinse gently. The roof comes back to its original color, and because the algae is dead rather than merely displaced, the result holds for a year and a half or more instead of a season. We tarp and pre-wet landscaping before we start and rinse it again when we finish.
