Pressure Washing in Fort Walton Beach, FL

Serving Fort Walton Beach, Okaloosa Island, Shalimar and Cinco Bayou with the same soft wash methods we use on the coast.

Your Local pressure washing Team in Fort Walton Beach

Fort Walton Beach has a broader mix of housing stock than the resort towns to the east, and that mix is the main thing that shapes our work here. Older block-and-stucco homes in the neighborhoods around Ferry Park and Cinco Bayou, mid-century ranches with painted brick, newer vinyl-sided construction, and the condo and rental stock out on Okaloosa Island. Each of those wants a different approach. Painted brick in particular is easy to ruin with pressure, and a lot of what we correct in this area is damage from previous cleanings rather than dirt.

Properties along Santa Rosa Sound and the Shalimar side of the bay get a persistent damp exposure. Morning fog off the water keeps north walls and shaded roof planes wet well into the day, and the result is heavy algae growth on roofs and a green film that returns quickly on the lower third of walls. We treat those elevations with a longer dwell so the growth is actually killed rather than rinsed pale. Fort Walton Beach also has a large military community around Eglin and Hurlburt, and we do a steady volume of move-out cleaning for base housing and rentals on short notice.

Because of Eglin and Hurlburt, this area runs on PCS timelines, and those do not move. We keep slots open for move-out and pre-inspection cleaning and can turn a house wash and driveway around quickly when a final walkthrough date is fixed. We also work regularly with realtors in the Fort Walton Beach market on pre-listing exterior cleaning, which routinely does more for curb appeal photos than anything else at the same price.
