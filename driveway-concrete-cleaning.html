Driveway & Concrete Cleaning in Destin, FL

Even, streak-free concrete with no zebra stripes, because we use a rotary surface cleaner instead of a wand.

Concrete, Pavers and Pool Decks Done Without Stripes

Concrete is the one surface where real pressure belongs, and it is also where most bad work shows up. Cleaning a driveway with a handheld wand leaves the wand marks behind: alternating light and dark bands, sometimes called zebra striping, that are permanently etched into the surface. We use commercial rotary surface cleaners that hold a fixed distance and sweep the slab at a consistent rate, so the whole surface lifts evenly. Edges, expansion joints and the transitions the surface cleaner cannot reach are detailed by hand afterward.

Different stains need different handling. Rust from irrigation and patio furniture needs a dedicated rust remover, not more pressure. Oil and transmission fluid need a degreaser and dwell time. Red clay, sand and the general gray film that builds up on Emerald Coast driveways come off with a pre-treatment and a proper surface cleaner pass. Paver driveways and pool decks get lower pressure so we are not blowing the joint sand out, and we can re-sand joints on request. For pool decks and lanais we are careful about runoff and rinse toward drains rather than into the pool.
