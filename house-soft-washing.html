House Soft Washing in Destin, FL

A full exterior wash that reaches the soffits, gutters and trim, not just the parts you can see from the driveway.

A Complete Exterior Wash, Sized to Your Siding

Siding on the Emerald Coast collects three different problems at once. Organic growth, meaning the green and black film of algae and mildew that thrives in our humidity. Salt and mineral deposits blown in off the Gulf and thrown up by irrigation. And ordinary road film and pollen. Each responds to something different, and the substrate underneath decides how much pressure is safe. Vinyl will warp and take on water behind the panels if you hit it hard. Stucco chips and spiders. Painted Hardie board and wood will lose their finish outright. That is why we treat house washing as a chemistry problem first and a pressure problem last.

Our house wash covers the full building envelope: siding on all elevations, soffits and fascia, gutter exteriors, window frames and trim, entry doors, columns, and the underside of eaves where spider webbing and mud daubers collect. We hand-treat stubborn areas around hose bibs and dryer vents rather than leaning on pressure to solve them. Windows are rinsed clean of solution so you are not left with spotting. Before we start, we ask you to close all windows and doors and we do a walk of the property with you to flag anything already damaged, so there are no surprises on either side.
