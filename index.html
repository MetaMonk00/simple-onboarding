About Us

Local Crews, Soft Wash Methods, Honest Pricing

Powerhouse Pressure Washing is a locally owned exterior cleaning company serving Destin and the surrounding Emerald Coast. We built the business on a simple idea: match the method to the surface. Shingles, painted siding and pavers each need a different approach, and blasting all of them at the same pressure is how property gets damaged. Our crews are trained, licensed and insured, and every job starts with a free walkthrough and a written price.
