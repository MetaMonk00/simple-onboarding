Our Story

About Powerhouse Pressure Washing

A local exterior cleaning company built around doing the job the right way instead of the fast way.

[ Photo: the Powerhouse team / team at work ]

Local Crews, Soft Wash Methods, Honest Pricing

Powerhouse Pressure Washing started in 2016 with one truck, one trailer and a short list of Destin neighbors who were tired of watching contractors etch their driveways and strip paint off their siding. The Gulf Coast is a demanding environment for exterior surfaces. Salt spray, ninety percent humidity through most of the summer, and near-constant sun mean algae, mold and mildew take hold quickly and come back fast if the underlying growth is never actually killed. Pressure alone knocks the color off a surface for a few weeks. It does not solve the problem.

So we built the company around soft washing. Low pressure, professional cleaning solutions, and enough dwell time to break down organic growth at the root before it is rinsed away. It takes longer than blasting a wall with a turbo nozzle, and it is the reason our results hold for a year or more rather than a season. Today we run multiple crews across Destin, Miramar Beach, Santa Rosa Beach, Fort Walton Beach and the rest of the Emerald Coast, handling everything from single-story homes to condo buildings, restaurant patios and HOA common areas. We are fully licensed and insured, we show up in the window we promised, and we give you a real number before we ever start.
